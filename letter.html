My Love ❤️

My love, you are finally crossing over into the age of a full adult, and I'm glad I'm here to be with you during it. It’s been a long time since we first started dating in 2021 and I'm so happy that I was able to realize that you were what I wanted from such a young age.

These four years we have had our ups and downs but it's clear to me at least that we have grown as a couple and are able to understand each other in a more emotional sense and I got to say I'm probably the luckiest guy to have met you, my soulmate at such a young age, and be able to experience life with you 🥰.

Now I've realized that I'm not the best with words and struggle writing notes like this, so I wanted to borrow some words and phrases that describe what I've come to learn and love by being with you.

You are my third relationship so far in life.

Starting with my first relationship, I thought love was a bright smile and pretty words. After what seemed like a brief instant of time, that relationship ended just as fast as it had started. As if love was like a vagabond, never stopping to settle.

In my second relationship, I thought I had come to know what love was just a little better. I thought that love must be happiness that arises from being with somebody. I soon came to find out that a relationship has more sides to it than happiness. They have their hardships too; jealousy, sadness, inconsideration, miscommunication, and of course heartbreak. At this point I didn’t even know what to think of love if it meant all these negative emotions.

That is until I found you, my love 🥰.

If I know what love is, it's because of you. Love isn't an emotion or an experience — it's a meshing together of two people. It's that sense of clarity and peace knowing your other half is with you. It’s that feeling of sunlight’s warmth on your skin. It's that feeling of tranquility and stillness you get when submerged in water.

It's a connection of hearts that builds upon each other's imperfections. It's the realization that without each other we’re incomplete.

This is what I've learned from being with you, my love.

I wish I had the ability to say all of this to you by word or that I was able to express my affection without these roadblocks I've built in my heart getting in the way. But I know that as we progress, I will get better at that, and one day I will be able to speak my heart out to you the way I am here.

I love you, my love 🥰🥰🥰

— Yours Always
